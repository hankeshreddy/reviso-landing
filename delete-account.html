Delete your account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Reviso account and study data, what we delete and what we must keep, for how long, and how to ask us to delete your account on your behalf.

Last updated: 29 August 2026 Applies to: Reviso app (Android & iOS)

01Delete your account in the app

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from inside the Reviso app:

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. On the “Delete account?” confirmation, tap Delete.

The full path is Profile → Delete account. You will be signed out immediately once deletion completes.

This cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period and no way to recover the account or its contents afterwards. If you only want to remove specific notes or chats, see Deleting individual items instead.

02Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app — for example you have lost your device or can no longer sign in — you can ask us to delete your account for you.

Email contact@reviso.in from the email address associated with your Reviso account, with the subject line “Delete my account”.

  • We may ask you to confirm a few details to verify that the account is yours before we act on the request.
  • We will complete the deletion and confirm it to you within the timeframes required by applicable law, and in any case within 30 days of verifying your request.
  • There is no charge for this, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

03What happens when you delete

When you confirm deletion in the app, your login is destroyed and the deletion of your data is triggered at the same time. Related records — your profile, notes, uploads, generated study material, schedules, quiz and focus history, and chat sessions — are removed automatically as part of the same operation.

Content stored as files (photos and scans of notes, PDFs you uploaded) is deleted from our file storage as part of the same process. Copies that remain only in encrypted backups are purged on the backup cycle described below.

04What data is deleted

Deleting your account removes the personal data we hold about you, including:

  • Account & profile — your email/login, display name, avatar emoji, target exam type, preparation style, and exam date.
  • Study content you provided — photos and scans of notes, PDFs, typed notes, subjects and topics you created, and the text extracted from your uploads by OCR.
  • AI-generated study materials — multiple-choice questions, flashcards, summaries, explanations, and the vector embeddings of your note text.
  • Learning activity — your revision schedule and history, review ratings and logs, spaced-repetition parameters, quiz attempts, answers, scores, focus (Pomodoro) sessions, streaks, XP, levels, and daily activity records.
  • AI chat — your chat sessions and messages, and any short-lived conversation context cache (which also expires on its own within about 30 minutes).
  • Notifications & preferences — your push notification token, reminder times, and notification settings.
  • Feedback — ratings and comments you left on AI-generated content.

Where we do not delete a record outright, we irreversibly anonymise it so it can no longer be linked to you.

05What is retained, and for how long

A limited set of records is kept after account deletion, either because the law requires it or because it cannot immediately be purged:

What Why it is kept Retention period
Personal data in general (profile, notes, uploads, generated materials, chat, activity) Time needed to complete deletion across systems Deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 90 days
Transaction, billing and tax records (for any Reviso Pro purchases) Legal and accounting obligations under Indian law Up to 8 years
Residual copies in encrypted backups Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule, not edited Purged within the backup cycle (rolling)
Diagnostic and AI usage logs (metadata and token counts only — not the text of your notes or messages) Security, debugging, and abuse prevention Up to 90 days
Aggregated or de-identified data Can no longer be used to identify you Retained

This mirrors the retention rules in our Privacy Policy.

06Deleting individual items

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove specific content. You can delete items one at a time and keep using Reviso:

Delete a note and its revision schedule

  • Open the note from your revision list to reach its detail screen.
  • Tap Delete Revision and confirm on the “Delete Revision?” prompt.
  • This permanently removes the note, its uploaded pages/files, its spaced-repetition schedule, and its review history. It cannot be undone.

Delete an AI chat session

  • In your AI chat history, press and hold the chat session you want to remove.
  • Tap Delete on the “Delete Chat” prompt.
  • This permanently removes that chat session and its messages.

Deleting individual items does not delete your account, your profile, or any other content. To remove everything, use Delete account.

07Questions

For any questions about account deletion or your data, contact us at contact@reviso.in. For the full picture of what we collect and how we handle it, see the Privacy Policy.
