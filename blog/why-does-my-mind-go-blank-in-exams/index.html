Why does my mind go blank in the exam?

Sab padha tha, phir bhi exam hall mein blank? Here is why, and how to stop it.

Reviso team4 Nov 20262 min read

⚡ Short answer

A blank mind in the exam usually has two causes: stress, which makes it harder for the brain to pull out stored answers, and study without recall practice, so the answer was never easy to retrieve in the first place. The fix is to practise the exam itself: answer questions from memory, under time, before the real day.

What actually happens in your brain

When you feel exam pressure, your body releases stress hormones. Short bursts of stress make it harder for the thinking part of the brain to work smoothly, so retrieving an answer feels like searching in a dark room (Arnsten, 2009). The answer is still stored. You just cannot reach it in that moment.

The second cause is how you studied. If you only read your notes, your brain is good at recognising the material but has rarely practised bringing it out. Under stress, weak paths are the first to fail.

Good news: practice protects memory from stress

In a study published in Science, students who learned by practising recall still remembered well under stress, while students who learned by re-reading did much worse under stress (Smith, Floerke & Thomas, 2016). In simple words: if you have practised pulling the answer out many times, stress cannot block it as easily.

3 ways to stop going blank

1. Test yourself every day, not just before the exam

Do 10 to 20 quick questions on what you studied. MCQs, flashcards, or simply writing answers on a blank page. Every successful recall makes the path stronger.

2. Practise in exam conditions

Once a week, sit at a table, set a timer, keep the phone away, and solve a past paper or mock test. Your brain learns that this situation is familiar, not dangerous.

3. In the hall: start with what you know

If you freeze, skip the question. Take three slow breaths and answer an easy one first. Getting a few marks calms the stress response, and the blocked answer often comes back.

Questions students ask

🤔Why do I remember answers right after the exam?

Once the stress drops, the brain can retrieve normally again. That shows the answer was stored; it was the pressure that blocked it.

⏳Does mock test practice really help?

Yes. Practising recall under exam-like conditions makes memory more resistant to stress on the real day.

🗣️What should I do if my mind goes blank during the exam?

Move on, breathe slowly a few times, answer easier questions first, and return later.
