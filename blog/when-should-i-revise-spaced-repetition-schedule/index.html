When should you revise a chapter?

Revision kab karein taaki bhoolein nahi? A simple schedule.

Reviso team4 Nov 20262 min read

⚡ Short answer

Revise a topic soon after you learn it, then again at growing gaps. A simple schedule many students use is 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days and 30 days after first studying it. Each revision should be a quick self-test, not a full re-read.

Why you need a schedule

More than a hundred years ago, Hermann Ebbinghaus showed that memory fades fast at first and then more slowly: the "forgetting curve". Each time you successfully recall something, the curve becomes flatter and the memory lasts longer. Revising at the right time means you catch the memory just before it fades.

A simple schedule

WhenWhat to doTime
Day 0Learn the topic properlyFull session
Day 1Write key points from memory, check gaps10–15 min
Day 310 quick questions10 min
Day 7Questions + fix weak points10 min
Day 14Mixed questions with other topics10 min
Day 30Final check before it goes long-term10 min

The exact gaps matter less than the idea: start close together, then spread out. If you get a topic wrong, bring the next review closer.

Common mistakes

  • Re-reading instead of testing. Revision should make you recall, not just look.
  • Only revising favourite subjects. Weak topics need the schedule most.
  • Keeping track in your head. After 20 topics, nobody remembers which is due when. Write it down or use an app.

Questions students ask

🤔How many times should I revise a chapter?

Around 4 to 5 short self-test revisions over a month is a good target for most topics.

⏳What if I miss a revision day?

Do it as soon as you can. A late revision is still far better than none.

🗣️Can I revise many subjects in one day?

Yes. Mixing short reviews of different topics in one day is normal with spaced repetition.
